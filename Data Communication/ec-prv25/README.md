# Extra Credit — Peer Review

**Course:** INFO 3312/5312 — Data Communication, Spring 2025

---

## What This Was

The extra credit assignment for INFO 3312/5312 was a peer review exercise. Students were given access to another team's project and asked to provide structured written feedback on their data communication choices: what worked, what could be clearer, and how their visualizations could be improved.

The submission format used Quarto with `format: typst` — rendered to PDF.

---

## The Peer Review Framework

Effective peer review in a data communication course isn't just "I liked it" or "the colors were good." The course emphasized evaluating:

- **Data-ink ratio** — is every visual element earning its place, or is there decoration that obscures the signal?
- **Encoding choices** — is the right channel being used for each variable (position, length, color, area)?
- **Audience awareness** — does the design work for someone encountering the data without prior context?
- **Annotation and labeling** — do titles, axis labels, and captions make the chart self-contained?
- **Accessibility** — does the color palette work for colorblind readers? Is the text large enough?
